What the event is

Code Kernel '26 is an online challenge on Unstop from 12 to 21 Oct, open to students from any college. Teams decode a real problem, build a solution and prove it works.

Why your work matters

Registrations decide whether the event succeeds, and every poster, message and reply brings in teams.

How we work
  1. Your lead gives you a clear task and a deadline. If anything is unclear, ask right away.
  2. Share your progress in your team group, not just at the end.
  3. Anything that goes public (posts, messages to CRs, sponsor messages) needs a lead's sign-off first.
  4. Be polite and quick with participants and sponsors, because you're representing X-Kernel.
  5. If you finish early or get stuck, tell your lead or Hasmitha, and we'll move you where help is needed.
What each team does

Publicity and Design bring people in. Database and the Help Desk look after the people who join. Event Mgmt and Training run the challenge itself. Documentation and Sponsorship handle approvals and partners.
